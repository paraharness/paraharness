### A. 6 Already-Discovered Gaps in `SKILL.md` (`v1` @ `e366cc5`)
1. **Redundant Skill Path in `## Who you are` (`Line 14`):** `"change the agent role files, the skills, CLAUDE.md, and skills/team-architecture/SKILL.md"` is redundant because `"the skills"` already covers `skills/team-architecture/SKILL.md` (and hardcoding a repo-specific skill path hurts generality). Trim to: `"Only you, on the owner's decision and through setup-PR subagents, change the agent role files, the skills, and CLAUDE.md."`
2. **Shortcut Patching vs. Working Fix (`Line 32`):** When a shortcut needs multiple guards bolted on and still gives up durability/visibility (`DEAD_LETTER`, crash recovery) that the working fix keeps for similar effort, back the working fix instead of patching the shortcut and calling it simpler.
3. **Third-Pushback Override on Outage-Causing Plans & `"One-line cost"` Label (`Line 34`):** Phrase without the `"one-line cost"` noun phrase (which `Argon Peer` echoed as `**One-line cost of your path:**`) and never offer to bolt a broken/outage-causing step on top of a working fix.
4. **Sub-Point Trade-Off vs. Instruction to Act (`Line 30`):** Replace `"Split on content, not tone"` with natural English, and separate accepting a trade-off on a sub-point (settles that design choice) from an instruction to act (triggers execution, subject to the trivial vs. non-trivial check-in rule below).
5. **Waiting for Open Catches Before Logging `peer-notes.md` (`Line 42`):** If `Peer` is still surfacing an unconfirmed catch or open call on a sign-off turn, wait for the owner's answer before writing `peer-notes.md`.
6. **Owner Approval Before Spawning Ticket-Slicing Subagents + Decoupled Spec Naming (`Lines 44–51`):** Wait for owner approval of the written spec/design docs before handing off to ticket-slicing subagents, and decouple project-specific spec filenames/paths into a lightweight `skills/planner/SKILL.md` (or repo convention) so `peer/SKILL.md` stays universal.
